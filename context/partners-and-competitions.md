# Partners and competitions

## Competition hosts

| Competition | Host | What students must make | Flara fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIRST LEGO League, Robofest | Planetarium Science Center, Bibliotheca Alexandrina | Innovation Project presentation; RoboArts / exhibition | Animated explainer |
| AAFFIAT | NTI (MCIT) with Telecom Egypt | Motion graphics, game design and storytelling, digital marketing tracks | Scenes, characters, trailers, music |
| Digitopia | MCIT / ITIDA | Gaming and Digital Arts track (Grade 4+) | Cutscenes, trailers, characters |
| WRO Future Innovators | WRO | 90-second team video | Pitch film |
| Technovation Girls | Technovation | 3-minute pitch and technical videos | Pitch and demo films |
| British Council Robotics | British Council Egypt | 3-minute team video | Team film |

Competitions allow any AI tool if disclosed, so Flara wins on fit, Arabic and safety, not by rule.

## What each side gets and gives

| Partner | Gets from Flara | Flara gets |
|---|---|---|
| Academies | A new course, co-branded certificates, better pricing as Authorized Academy, right to host qualifiers | Paying customers, trained coaches, students every season |
| Competitions | Competition Pass for teams, Best Film award, AI-use disclosure kit, workshops, event films, rights protection for official footage | Official Creative AI Partner status, reach, a place in the yearly calendar |
