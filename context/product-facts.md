# Flara: product facts

Sources: flara.io home page (read 26 Oct 2026), pricing pasted by Ibrahim (22 Oct 2026). The flara.io pricing and Terms pages returned 404 to automated fetches.

## Live today

Flara lists nine studios:

1. Animated Short Films: idea → cast → scenes → render; "characters, scenes, and styles that stay consistent"
2. AI Music Generation
3. Ads Generation
4. Video Translation
5. Video Highlights
6. Text-to-Speech
7. Face Swap (must be off for student accounts)
8. Image to Video (students draw, Flara animates)
9. URL to Video

Also: Flara MCP (connect ChatGPT or Claude to a Flara account).

Models named on the site: Google Veo 3 / 3.1, Runway Gen-3, Pika Labs, Seedance 1.5 Pro / 2.0, Grok Imagine, Kling 2.1 / 2.6, ByteDance V1 Pro. Their terms set the effective minimum age (13+ for mainstream providers) and matter for cross-border data rules.

## What game-design students can use today vs roadmap

| Need | Flara | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Story and game world | Story drafting | Live |
| Characters and art style | Consistent characters, scenes, styles | Live |
| Scenes, cutscenes, trailers | Animated Short Films | Live |
| Animate student drawings | Image to Video | Live |
| Soundtrack and voices | AI music, text-to-speech, translation | Live |
| Sprites, backgrounds, tilesets for Scratch, Pygame, Pygame Zero, HTML/CSS | Asset export | Roadmap |
| Sound effects | Short SFX | Roadmap |
