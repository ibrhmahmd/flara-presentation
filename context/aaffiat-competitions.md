## 1. Film Festival (most relevant to Flara)
*Source: Film Festival Rules.pdf (February folder)*

- **Name:** "Film Festival Competition"; Arabic title "قواعد مسابقة مهرجان السينما" (Film Festival Competition Rules). The festival name is still a template placeholder: "[Name of festival]".
- **Age:** 9-16, from Egypt, counted at the time of submission. Team size: not stated (the score sheet asks for a single "Filmmaker's Name"). Age groups: the score sheet has an "Age Group" field, but no groups are defined.
- **Timeline:** organisers will set submission deadlines "with sufficient lead time". Films are screened on competition day, and the screening time is announced after submissions close. No dates are given; the folder name implies February 2027.
- **What to submit:** a film of **3-10 minutes** as MP4 or MOV, delivered on USB or cloud storage. It must come with a **logline** (one sentence that describes the film) and fit one category:
  1. **Biographical Films** (documentary): the lives, discoveries and legacies of Arab scientists.
  2. **Innovation and Impact** (documentary or educational content): the innovations of Arab scientists and their impact on the world.
  3. **Inspiration in Community** (short film or ads): stories of community initiatives and grassroots projects.
- **Judging (score sheet, out of 100):** Creativity 30 (originality of story and concept; props, visuals, settings). Storytelling 30 (plot, character development, clarity of message, audience engagement). Technical Execution 40, split as Camera work 20 (framing, shots, movement, **sound and music**), Editing and Pacing 10, and Visual Effects 10 (if applicable). Judges also write feedback on strengths, areas to improve and encouragement.
- **Originality:** films must be "primarily created by young filmmakers, with minimal adult assistance"; kids should be hands-on in all aspects. Students **may use some shots from other movies**. Original content is encouraged, and any material the student did not create must be attributed, with an explanation of how it influenced the film.
- **AI rules:** not stated. Language: not stated (the rules are bilingual).
- **Tools:** not stated.
- **Before/after phases:** the logline is required up front (pre-production). The pipeline is submission, then selection, then a live screening on competition day. The Audience Choice award implies a public screening.
- **Prizes:** every selected film that meets the standard gets a completion certificate. Awards: Best Biographical Film, Best Science Simplification, Best Innovation and Impact Film, Audience Choice, **Best Storytelling**, Best Editing, and Best Film (1st place only). Non-compliant films are rejected.
- **Organisers/sponsors:** not stated.

## 2. Game to Gain ("الجيم تو جين")
*Source: Game to gain competition.pdf (February folder)*

- **What it is:** "الجيم تو جين" is **"Game to Gain"**, a student **video-game-making** competition. It is not generative AI.
- **Name:** "Game to Gain Competition". No Arabic title appears in the file.
- **Entry:** individuals or teams of **up to 6**. Categories: **Junior 8-13, Senior 14+, Professional "any age less than 25"**.
- **Topics:** Creative story, Mind game, Sport game, **Arab scientist game**.
- **Tools (examples only):** laptop or PC; Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender; **Scratch for Junior only**.
- **What to submit:** the game itself. Students must also **send a video of the game before the competition date**. The file gives no format or length for the video.
- **Judging (award nominations, no weights):** Excellence in Idea; **Visual Art** (appearance and visual effects); **Audio** (music and sound effects); Design (mechanics, level design, difficulty balance); Best Student Game (overall); **Audience Award** (public vote); **Best Marketing Award** (based on the game's marketing plan). Finalists are evaluated by a "Finalist Committee" of jurors appointed by "the Sponsor": professional and indie game designers and video-game specialists.
- **AI, originality, copyright, language:** not stated.
- **Timeline:** not stated, beyond the pre-competition video and the folder month of February 2027.
- **Before/after phases:** *before the game*: the idea, the story topic, visual art and audio. *Around/after the game*: the **gameplay video sent before the event**, the **marketing plan** (its own award), and the public vote. These are the clearest openings for Flara: story drafting, character art, music, trailer/promo video and translation.
- **Prizes:** the awards listed above. Prize values: not stated. Sponsor: unnamed.

## 4. AAFFIAT Entrepreneur competition
*Source: AAFFIAT Entrepreneur competition.pdf (May folder; the text is the **2024** edition)*

- **Entry:** ages 13-25. Teams of 2-4, with at least one adult monitor (a later line says "no more than 5"). Categories: **Idea** (no prototype) or **Pitch** (prototype required).
- **Timeline (2024 text):** online registration at www.africaniat.com by June 1, 2024. The top 20 teams attend the physical final on July 15, 2024. 2027 dates: not stated.
- **What to submit:** a pitch video of **4 minutes or less (240 s)**, uploaded with the application form; longer videos are not judged. It must cover:
  - the product idea, with research evidence and a visual of the prototype;
  - the target customer persona, including a picture or collage;
  - the market segment and competitors;
  - the marketing plan (pricing, distribution, promotion, brand identity, **an advertisement or promotional piece**, a media plan);
  - 40-60 customer interviews;
  - partnerships.
- **Video guidance:** "present as if speaking to investors... **storytelling techniques**"; "**Animation, soundtrack, and/or any special effects may be used**"; art direction, style, tone and sound should suit the target customer; voice volume must be consistent.
- **Judging (no weights):** Creativity and Innovation, Business Model Canvas, Finance and Production, Marketing and Market Research, Communications, Partnership.
- **Prizes:** the top 5 of the 20 finalists win awards. Values: not stated.
- **AI rules:** not stated.

## 5. Robot Art Hero (dance and painting robots)
*Source: Hero Art pdf.pdf (February folder)*

- **Entry:** ages 6-9, teams of up to 6. Themes: paint, music, dance. Robot size up to 35x35 cm; any kit.
- **Dancing robot:**
  - an autonomous performance of 1-2 minutes, synced to music the team chooses; no violent, military or unethical content;
  - a 15-minute interview.
  - Score /80: Design 10, Sensors 5, Programming 15, **Evidence of authenticity 5**, **Choreography and storytelling 10**, Use of technology 5, Entertainment 10, Judge's discretion 15.
- **Painting robot:**
  - the robot must paint with a physical brush (no ink-jet printing);
  - 4 trials of 2 minutes each, drawing two cards (Hat, Smiley Face, Car), 30 points each;
  - 20 points for the technical meeting, plus a 10-point bonus for finishing in under 2 minutes.
- **Flara angle:** weak. The only link is music and story for the dance. AI rules, timeline and prizes: not stated.

## 6. Little Einstein (kindergarten)
*Source: little Einstein competition.pdf = little Einstein 2026.pdf (February folder)*

- **Entry:** ages 5-7, groups of 3-6.
- **Task:** the children program a robot to travel from a rocket start square to their favourite planet. They then present the planet to the judge: its name, type, whether it can support life, its size and its position in the solar system. The robot helps them "continue a story".
- **Rubric, timeline, prizes, AI rules:** not stated. Weak fit for Flara, apart from a possible story or visual aid for the planet presentation.

## 7. Software and research competitions (low direct fit)

- **Leader of Software** (*Leader of Software.pdf*; Arabic version *Software Rules.pdf*, titled "مسابقة قادة السوفت وير", "Software Leaders competition"):
  - **Task:** any software solution (web, mobile, ML, platform) that uses **World Bank Data Catalog** datasets. Themes: sport management, risk/crisis, healthcare, agriculture/food security, education, business development.
  - **Ages:** Junior 9-15 and Senior 16-24 per the English file; the Arabic file says Junior 10-15. Entry is individual or team. Beginner and Professional levels.
  - **Judging:** idea 1/3, implementation (UX, design, performance) 1/3, impact 1/3. The Arabic rubric scores each on 1-3 / 4-6 / 7-10 bands.
  - **Rules:** the Arabic file excludes hateful, defamatory or illegal content and content that violates others' rights such as privacy.
  - **Prizes:** cash prizes, or a place in the AAFFIAT freelancer hub.
- **AAFFIAT Inventions** (*Iam inventor competition.pdf*):
  - **Entry:** Junior 11-16, Senior 17-23, organisations.
  - **Themes:** humanoid welcome robots (Senior only), smart home automation, cultural-preservation technologies.
  - **Stages:** a **film of up to 5 minutes** plus documents emailed for the first evaluation, then a final of 10 minutes of presentation and 10 minutes of jury Q&A.
  - **Originality/IP:** entries must be the participants' original work or licensed to them; participants secure their own IP; organisers may use entry images and video for evaluation.
  - **Judging:** 14 criteria including Documentation and Presentation. No weights.
- **Medtech summit, HealthInnovate challenge** (*SHS Healthcare Innovation Competition.pdf*):
  - **Entry:** ages 14-22 plus researchers, teams of up to 3.
  - **Themes:** digital medical records, healthcare data, telehealth kits.
  - **Judging:** 5 equal criteria, including **Presentation Quality ("audio and visual quality... convincing pitch")**.
- **Physics of Life research** (*research physics category.pdf*):
  - **Entry:** ages 12-18 (Junior 12-14, Senior 15-18), up to 3 per team.
  - **Deliverables:** a 10-15-page report, a poster, and an oral presentation with interview.
  - **Weights:** Question 10%, Design 15%, Execution 20%, Creativity 20%, Presentation and Interview 35%.
  - **Rules:** help received must be documented.
- **Scientific Research: Math and Biology** (*research mathematics and biology categories pdf.pdf*):
  - **Entry:** ages 11-18 (Middle 11-13, High 14-18).
  - **Deliverables:** a report plus a poster or digital slides; an adult sponsor is required.
  - **Weights:** Question 20, Method 30, Analysis 30, Communication 20.
  - **Rules:** plagiarism or fabrication means disqualification.
- **Social Science Research** (*Guidelines Social Science Research.pdf*):
  - **Entry:** Junior 11-14 (individual or groups of up to 6), Senior 15-18.
  - **Deliverables:** a single PDF paper.
  - **Weights /100:** Purpose 20, Method 25, Results 20, Originality 20, Communication 15.
  - **Rules:** plagiarism, including self-plagiarism, means disqualification.
- **Cyber Secure Heroes** (*cypersecurity competition rules.pdf*, *Cybersecurity competition .pdf*):
  - **Entry:** Junior 13-16, Senior 17-21, Pro 21+, teams of 2-4.
  - **Format:** 5 levels of 60 minutes each in a simulated environment, plus a logbook.
  - **Scoring:** completion 70, collaboration 10, time 20.
- **Young Innovators** (*Young innovators competition.pdf*):
  - **Entry:** Junior 11-16, Senior 17-25, 26 and over.
  - **Themes:** green energy, natural-disaster recovery, education, precision agriculture.
  - **Deliverable:** a 5-10-minute presentation.
  - **Rubric /100:** a business-plan style rubric, including IP (copyright and trademarks) at 10.
- **Sus- mobile application competition:** image-only PDF, not read.

## 8. Robotics games (no Flara fit)

- **Relay Robot** (*Robot_Hero_Relay_Robo.pdf*): ages 10-15, teams of 3-6. Autonomous line-following relay on a 2 m x 2 m mat; alliances are drawn by lottery 2 weeks before the event.
- **Bowling Robot** (*Bowling robot competition.pdf*): Junior 10-14, Senior 15-22. Autonomous; 3 bowling frames.
- **Robot soccer, Afro-Asian League** (*Robot soccer.pdf*): Junior 10-15, Senior 16-21, Masters 22+, teams of 3-6. Remote-controlled; ready-made kits are not allowed.
- **Flag collector** (*Flag collector competition.pdf*): ages 10-17, teams of 2-6. 3:30 runs; the Palestine flag scores 40, the other flags 20.
- **Rescue** (*Rescue Competition rules.pdf*): autonomous earthquake-rescue robot, 3-minute rounds. Ages: not stated.
- **RUV, Rescue Underwater Vehicle** (*RUV Competition.pdf*, 2024 text):
  - **Teams:** 5-25 including mentors; everything must be written in English.
  - **Fee:** 7,000 EGP.
  - **Scoring:** tasks 100, safety 30, technical report of 15-25 pages 100, 15-minute presentation 50.
  - **2024 timeline:** registration 9 Mar-20 Jun 2024, report due 1 Jul, regional finals 12-13 Jul, international finals in Saudi Arabia in Sep 2024.
  - **Prizes:** 2nd place 2,000 LE and 3rd place 1,000 LE in the professional category.
