# Audiences and messaging

## Rights Hero leadership (internal)
- Message: apply LEGO Education's model. The calendar (competition seasons) is the lock-in, not the product. Sell the season, not the seat.
- Frame as brand and talent investment. No market-size numbers.
- Show the LEGO canvas, Flara's canvas, the partnership plan, guardrails and decisions needed.

## Tech principal
- Message: my proposal for Flara's business model and partnerships, my vision, the opportunity, how to establish Flara.
- Vision: Flara becomes the creative studio Egyptian students use for competition films and game projects, the way LEGO kits are the tool for robotics.

## Academy owner
- Problem: students take months of courses to get a working game that still looks basic; story and design get little time; parents can't judge code.
- Insight: **parents pay and sit in the audience; they judge what they see.**
- Flara: story first, then code (story → characters → world → mechanics → code → trailer). Coding stays at the core; Flara adds story, art and film.
- Gains: a new course to sell, stronger showcase days, films families share (free marketing), a clear difference from other academies.
- Ask: co-design a "Game Storytelling with AI" pilot course; follow-up meeting.

## Competition director
- Problem: game entries look alike; time goes to code; parents, sponsors and media can't follow.
- Gains: a new category, finals people want to watch, fair AI judging (disclosure kit), official event films.
- Ask: a Game Storytelling category (game + trailer, judged on story, design, presentation); follow-up meeting. Flara is offered, not required, so the competition stays neutral.

## Do and don't (external)
- Do: lead with the student as director; show their input in the demo; bring a backup trailer.
- Do: keep "new opportunities in image, video, film generation" short and concrete (skills: storytelling, art direction, AI filmmaking).
- Don't: say students don't need coding.
- Don't: mention game-asset export.
- Don't: promise protection for purely AI-generated work.
