# Ibn Battuta · Film 2: Closing film "Journey complete"

Studio: **Film Generation → Advanced Mode** (not Quick Film: Quick Film writes its own characters and scenes, so the style can't be controlled).

Every scene in Flara is a fixed **8-second clip**; the shortest film is **1 minute**, so each film has 7 scenes (56 s).

The closing mirrors the opening: birds leaving the nest (Film 1, scene 5), and a bird returning (scene 5 here).

**Narration:** both diary lines are his own words (Rihla, Gibb IV p. 925 and p. 977). The first two short framing sentences are ours.

**Story Summary**

```
The ending film for the children's game "The Adventures of Ibn Battuta". After almost thirty years of travel across Africa, Arabia, Persia, India and China, the traveller Ibn Battuta, now about 45, comes home to Fez, Morocco, in 1349. He walks through the gate of Fez at golden hour, lays down his wooden travelling staff, and watches a bird return to its nest: the opposite of the day he left Tangier. In 1355 he tells his story to a young scholar, Ibn Juzayy, who writes it down; the pages become a book, the Rihla. Calm, warm, historically accurate, no dialogue: a narrator reads lines from the Rihla.
```

**Narration (voice-over text)**

```

وأقمت في كنف إحسانه بعد طول الرحلة.
```
