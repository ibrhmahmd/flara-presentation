# Ibn Battuta · Video Translation and Dubbing

Two studios can make the English versions. Neither has a prompt field: you set only the fields below.

## Option A. Video Translation (recommended for TTS narration)

Studio: **Video Translation**. The AI dubs the audio and makes subtitles; you can download the **dubbed video** and an **SRT subtitle file**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Video File** | the exported Arabic MP4 (Film 1, Film 2, or a city intro with narration) |
| **Target Language** | English |
| **Translation Title** | e.g. Ibn Battuta Film 1 EN |

**Use the SRT for the judges:** keep the Arabic video and add the English subtitles. The Arabic stays the main version, because the game's scrolls and title are in Arabic. Use the dubbed English video for the audience vote and social media.

## Option B. Video Dubbing (if Saleh records the narration)

Studio: **AI Video Dubbing**, which dubs into another language *in the speaker's own voice*. Use it when the Arabic narration is a student's real voice: the English version then keeps his voice.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Video File** | the Arabic MP4 with the student's recorded narration |
| **Target Language** | English |
| **Title** | e.g. Ibn Battuta Film 1 EN (Saleh) |


Check the English subtitles against the English quotes in `4-text-to-speech.md`: names must read Ibn Battuta, Tangier, Cairo, Damascus, Fez, Timbuktu, Ibn Juzayy and the Rihla.
