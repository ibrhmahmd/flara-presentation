# Ibn Battuta · AI Music

Studio: **Music Generation**. Two steps: a **Music Project** (album details), then **Tracks**. Each track generation gives **two takes**: keep the better one and note it ("take A/B"), as the game already does. A track can be **extended** if it's too short.

Already made with Flara and kept in the game: **"Cairo - Sailing the Nile"** and **"Damascus - Mender of Hearts"** (36 s loops, take A). The settings below keep the new tracks in the same family.

Export the audio into the game's `music/` folder. This is the Flara output that goes **into the game**, for the **Excellence in Audio** award.

## Step 3. Sound effects

Flara has no separate sound-effects studio: SFX come from the music studio with short prompts. Make one more project, **"Ibn Battuta SFX"** (Genre Ambient, Mood Peaceful, Number of Tracks 9), use the same Avoid Tags and Instrumental ON, then **trim the best 0.5–2 s in an audio editor** (e.g. Audacity) and save it as `.wav` or `.ogg` for Pygame.

| # | Title | Style | Prompt | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | SFX Item Pickup | oud, pluck, short | `One single bright pluck on an oud string with a tiny shimmer, then silence. Very short.` | All |
| 7 | SFX Level Complete | qanun, frame drum, fanfare | `A short triumphant two-second phrase on qanun with one bendir hit, then silence.` | All |
| 8 | SFX Gate Opens | foley, wood, creak | `A heavy old wooden door creaking slowly open on iron hinges, no music.` | Tangier |
| 9 | SFX River Splash | foley, water | `A single small splash into a river, no music.` | Cairo |
| 10 | SFX Dish Breaks | foley, ceramic | `A porcelain dish shattering on a stone floor, no music.` | Damascus |
| 11 | SFX Mender of Hearts | chime, gentle | `A gentle warm chime, two notes rising, then silence.` | Damascus |
| 12 | SFX Tile Match | wood, bell, short | `A soft wooden click followed by one light bell, then silence.` | Fez |
| 13 | SFX Sandstorm | ambient, wind | `Desert wind rising with blowing sand for two seconds, no music.` | Timbuktu |
| 14 | SFX Water Skin | foley, water, chime | `Water sloshing in a leather water skin, then a soft relieved chime.` | Timbuktu |

The music model is built for songs, so foley sounds (door, splash, dish) may come out musical. If a take doesn't sound right after two tries, mark it "not Flara" and use a free SFX library instead. Don't count it as Flara output.
