## 1. Opening and Tangier

Text (Gibb I p. 8):

```
فحزمتُ أمري على هجر الأحباب، وفارقتُ وطني مفارقةَ الطيور للوكور
```

Voice / style field:

```
VOICE: Arabic, Modern Standard with a classical reading. A warm young male voice, slow and calm, like reading from an old travel diary. Clear tashkeel pronunciation, a short pause at each comma. No music, no effects.
```

*English:* "I braced my resolution to leave all my dear ones, and left my home as birds leave their nests."
