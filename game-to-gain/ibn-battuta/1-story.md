## Story prompt

```
Write the story for a short animated film for the children's game "The Adventures of Ibn Battuta". The hero is silent; a narrator reads short lines from his real travel diary, the Rihla, in Arabic.

STORY: In June 1325, aged 21, Ibn Battuta leaves his home city of Tangier, Morocco, alone, to make the Hajj to Mecca. Leaving his parents hurts. A trip meant to last months becomes almost thirty years of travel across Africa and Asia. He sails the Nile to Cairo, finds kindness in Damascus during Ramadan, studies and later returns to Fez, and crosses the Sahara to Timbuktu. In 1349 he comes home to Fez, lays down his travelling staff, and in 1355 tells his story to the young scholar Ibn Juzayy, who writes it down as the Rihla.

TONE: adventure and wonder, like a first-person travel diary. Historically accurate; no invented magic, villains or romance. Short scenes, simple language for ages 10–16.

CHARACTER (keep identical in every shot): Ibn Battuta, a young Moroccan traveller about 21 years old, as on the attached start-screen image: short dark beard, white turban, long brown wool cloak over a light tunic, leather sandals, travel backpack, wooden travelling staff in his right hand, kind curious eyes.

ART STYLE (must follow): 2D animation in detailed 16-bit pixel art, exactly matching the attached game screenshots of "The Adventures of Ibn Battuta". Crisp square pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no 3D, no realistic or painterly rendering. Dark brown pixel outlines (#31211d). Warm sunny palette only: light sand #f3c77e, warm sand #d49f4c, ochre #c2843a, leather brown #865c38, deep sea blue #124778, sky blue #40a1ef, teal #3d8985, olive green #6b7a41, parchment #ebd3aa. Side-view and wide shots, slow gentle camera moves. Historically accurate 14th-century Islamic world: no modern objects, no text, letters or subtitles on screen, no watermark. Calm, wonder-filled mood for children aged 10–16.
```
