## For the Best Marketing award

The award judges the **marketing plan**, not only the ad. The team writes a one-page plan: who the players are (from the Target audience above), the message (help the crab, keep the sea clean), the channels (the academy's page, parents' WhatsApp groups, the itch.io page, the event's audience vote), the launch date, and this ad in both formats.
