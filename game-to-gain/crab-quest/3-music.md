# Crab Quest · Music and sound effects

Studio: **Music Generation**. Today the game uses **one track** (`music/background_music.mp3`) for every chapter. This pack gives a menu theme plus **one theme per chapter**, then the sound effects. It's the Flara output that goes **into the game**, for the **Excellence in Audio** award ("music and sound effects").

Each generation gives **two takes**: keep the better one and note it (take A/B). Extend a track if it's too short. Save the exports in the game's `music/` folder.

## Step 3. Sound effects

Flara has no separate sound-effects studio: SFX come from short music prompts. Make a second project, **"Crab Quest SFX"** (Genre Electronic, Mood Happy, Number of Tracks 10), with Instrumental ON and the same Avoid Tags. Then **trim the best 0.5–2 s** in an audio editor (e.g. Audacity) and export each as `.wav` or `.ogg` for Pygame Zero. Add the Style `retro game sound effect, 8-bit, short` to every SFX track.

| # | Title | Prompt | Where in the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | SFX Jump | `One short bouncy 8-bit jump sound, rising pitch, then silence.` | Jump (levels 1, 4, 5, 6) |
| 9 | SFX Food Pickup | `One short bright bubbly pop with a tiny sparkle, then silence.` | Collect algae (level 4) |
| 10 | SFX Bird Hit | `A short squawk-like retro game hit sound, falling pitch, then silence.` | Hit by a bird (level 2) |
| 11 | SFX Stuck in Plastic | `A short muffled plastic crinkle with a sad descending 8-bit tone.` | "STUCK IN PLASTIC!" (level 3) |
| 12 | SFX Caught | `A short dramatic retro game-over sting, three falling notes.` | "CAUGHT!" (level 5) |
| 13 | SFX Wave | `A gentle ocean wave rushing onto the sand, two seconds, no music.` | "THE WAVE IS COMING!" (level 2) |
| 14 | SFX Bubbles | `Soft underwater bubbles rising, two seconds, no music.` | Ambient (levels 3–6) |
| 15 | SFX Level Win | `A short happy 8-bit victory jingle on marimba, two seconds.` | "WINNER!" (all levels) |
| 16 | SFX Turtle Appears | `A warm magical chime, three rising notes, then silence.` | The turtle arrives (level 3) |
| 17 | SFX Reunion | `A short joyful fanfare on steel drums with a happy final note.` | "REUNITED! YOU ARE HOME." (level 6) |

The music model is built for songs, so foley sounds (wave, crinkle) may come out musical. If a take doesn't sound right after two tries, mark it "not Flara" and use a free SFX library instead. Don't count it as Flara output.

## Wiring into the game (team's code)

Pygame Zero plays music with `music.play('name')` and sounds with `sounds.name.play()`. Music files go in `music/` and sound files in `sounds/`, with lowercase names and no spaces (e.g. `music/chapter_3.mp3`, `sounds/jump.wav`). Writing this code is the team's work, not Flara's.
