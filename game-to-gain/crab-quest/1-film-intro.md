# Crab Quest · Film 1: Story intro "Caught!"

Studio: **Film Generation → Advanced Mode** (not Quick Film, which picks its own characters and style). Every scene is a fixed **8-second clip**; the shortest film is **1 minute**, so this film has **7 scenes (56 s)**.

The film plays before Chapter 1 and sets up the whole game: a crab is caught on the beach, ends up in a kitchen, and decides to escape home to the sea. It also plants the message about plastic in the sea, which pays off in Chapter 3.

Compare every frame with the game art in `partners/media/flara-inputs/crab-quest/` (`crab_medium.png`, `level_1_bg.jpg`, `level_2_bg.jpg`, `level_6_bg.png`).

**Story Summary**

```
The story intro for the children's game "Crab Quest". A small red crab lives happily with its family in a colourful coral reef. One day it wanders to a busy beach, where a fishing net catches it and it ends up in a bucket in a kitchen, next to a pot on the stove. Through the kitchen window the crab sees the sea far away. It remembers its family and decides to escape and find the way home, past birds, plastic pollution and predator fish. Hopeful and brave, never scary; a calm narrator tells the story in English. The message: the sea is the crab's home, so keep it clean.
```

**Narration (voice-over text)**

```
Deep in a colourful reef lived a little red crab and its family.
One sunny day, the little crab wandered too far, all the way to a busy beach.
Suddenly, a net! The crab was caught, and carried far away from the sea.
Now it sits in a bucket in a noisy kitchen. But through the window, it can see the waves.
The sea is home. Its family is waiting.
The little crab makes a brave plan: escape, and find the way home.
Help the crab on its quest!
```
