# Flara: Proposed Business Model and Partnerships

## Purpose

I propose that Flara grows through partnerships with Egyptian STEM academies and student competitions, using the business model LEGO Education built around its kits.

- **For:** our tech principal
- **What it covers:** my vision for Flara, the business model canvas I propose, the partners we can work with, and how we establish Flara in this market
- **What I am asking:** agreement on the direction, and support for a first pilot with academies and competitions

## My vision for Flara

Flara becomes the creative studio Egyptian students use for competition films and game projects, the way LEGO kits are the tool for robotics.

- **Today:** Flara sells individual plans to creators on flara.io.
- **Tomorrow:** academies and competitions bring Flara to students every season, in Arabic and English.
- **Why it matters:** students who learn to create with Flara stay with it, and Rights Hero builds its brand with the next generation of creators.

## The model we learn from: LEGO Education

LEGO Education gives the curriculum away, charges for the kit, and uses competitions to make schools buy again every season.

1. **Free curriculum and software** get teachers to adopt LEGO.
2. **The paid kit** is the paywall, about $85 per student.
3. **A new Annual Set** comes with each season's theme.
4. **Competitions** such as FIRST LEGO League give teams a reason to enrol every year.
5. **Platform changes** (SPIKE to the new CS & AI kit) make teams buy new kits.

Academies and certified trainers earn from running courses on the kit, so they sell LEGO for LEGO. From August 2027 LEGO runs its own competition, LEGO League, after leaving FIRST.

## The opportunity

Egyptian competitions and academies already ask students for films, scenes and game materials, and Flara makes most of them today.

| Where | What students must make | What Flara gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WRO Future Innovators | 90-second team video | Pitch film |
| Technovation Girls | 3-minute pitch and technical videos | Pitch and demo films |
| FIRST LEGO League | Innovation Project presentation | Animated explainer |
| AAFFIAT (NTI) | Motion graphics, game design and storytelling tracks | Scenes, characters, trailers, music |
| Digitopia (ITIDA) | Gaming and Digital Arts track | Cutscenes, trailers, characters |
| Academy game-design courses | Characters, art styles, scenes, game assets for Scratch, Pygame, Pygame Zero, HTML/CSS | Characters, styles, scenes and music today; asset export is roadmap |

Competitions allow any AI tool if its use is disclosed, so Flara wins on fit, Arabic support and safety, not by rule.

## Potential partnerships

Two partner types come first: academies, who pay and teach, and competitions, who give Flara visibility and a reason to use it.

| Partner | Examples | What they get from Flara | What Flara gets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Academies | Techno Future, Big Hero, iSchool, 3C, Engineering For Kids | A new course to sell, co-branded certificates, better pricing as an Authorized Academy, the right to host qualifiers | Paying customers, trained coaches, students every season |
| Competitions | Planetarium Science Center (FLL, Robofest), NTI (AAFFIAT), ITIDA (Digitopia) | Free Competition Pass for teams, a Best Film award, an AI-use disclosure kit for judges, event films, rights protection for official footage | Official Creative AI Partner status, reach to every team, a place in the yearly calendar |

Academy tiers carry no exclusivity. Schools come later, through the academies that already run school clubs.

## How we establish Flara

We start with a small pilot in academies and competitions, then reach schools through the academies, then go direct.

| Phase | When | What | Starts when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Academies, competitions | 2026–27 season | Partner with AAFFIAT, FLL, Robofest and Digitopia; 3–5 pilot academies; first Season Pack | Licences filed |
| 2. Schools via academies | 2027–28 season | Academy-run school clubs; Game Design Pack; game asset export; first Flara film challenge | Pilot reviewed |
| 3. Schools direct | 2028 onward | Direct school plans; national challenge with a ministry or ITIDA | Phase 2 proven |

## What we need to make it work

Three product gaps and four guardrails stand between this plan and a first pilot.

**Product gaps**

- An academy plan: today only Enterprise fits academies, since the other plans are for individuals
- Game asset export: sprites, backgrounds and sound effects for Scratch, Pygame, Pygame Zero and HTML/CSS
- A student safe mode: teacher-managed accounts, an AI-use log, and Face Swap switched off

**Guardrails**

- Egypt's data-protection law (151/2020), enforceable from 31 October 2026: a licence and a cross-border transfer licence before any student joins
- Written guardian consent for students under 15
- A minimum age: the AI models behind Flara set 13+, so younger students need a teacher-run mode
- Copyright: purely AI-generated work is likely unprotected under Law 82/2002, so students should draw, write and direct, with Flara animating
