## Decisions

- Storytelling is the thread that runs through every chapter now; a dedicated Storytelling category is proposed for Phase 4.
- AI rules: the forum's rules don't mention AI, so we both show how work stays student-made and propose a tool-neutral AI-use policy and disclosure kit.
- Game to Gain may claim: story, world, consistent characters from sprite descriptions, soundtrack and sound effects (via music prompts), trailer and ad. Image generation only as "coming soon". Never claim export into a game engine.
- Phases: 1 instructors, 2 student pilot (2 academies, 20 students, ~10 projects, students choose the competition), 3 May round, 4 full season. Metrics: students trained, projects submitted, coaches certified. Periods (8 Nov): Nov instructors, Dec–Jan student projects, Feb round, May on grow. Courses are a separate project built after a requirements session; in Nov instructors learn Flara and how it fits each competition.
- Accounts: instructor vs student accounts is an option to decide together with the partners (backups).
- Pricing: no pricing slide (removed 8 Nov). Forum offers (event films, awards, workshops) are for Flara management; not in the deck yet.
- Data protection, age and Face Swap appear in the speaker notes only.
