# Partners deck: context

One deck for **academy owners** and **competition directors**. Separate meetings, short deck plus a live demo. Shared facts live in `../context/`.

## Flow
- **Core (1–7):** cover, problem, audience, approach, what students make, live demo, AI use.
- **For academies (8–9):** gains, then a pilot course proposal.
- **For competitions (10–11):** gains, then a category proposal.
- **Close (12–13):** next step, contact.

In a given meeting, skip the other audience's section. Press End or type `#12` in the URL to jump.

## Message
- Parents pay and sit in the audience; they judge what they see. For competitions, sponsors and media are in the hall as well.
- Story first, then code. Coding stays at the core; Flara adds the story, art and film around it.
- The student directs; they draw, Flara animates; every AI step is logged.

## Asks
- Academy: co-design a "Game Storytelling with AI" pilot class, then a follow-up meeting.
- Competition: an optional Game Storytelling category (game + trailer), then a follow-up meeting. Flara is offered, not required.

## Don't
- Say students don't need coding.
- Mention game-asset export (it's roadmap).
- Promise rights protection for purely AI-generated work.
