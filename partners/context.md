The first pitch to the **Afro-Asian Forum (AAFFIAT) organisers and academy owners, together in one meeting**. Competition facts come from `../context/aaffiat-competitions.md` (the forum's Drive files). Shared product facts are in `../context/`.

No live demo: the example videos are the proof. Briefs are in `media/briefs.md`.

## Decisions

- Storytelling is the thread that runs through every chapter now; a dedicated Storytelling category is proposed for Phase 4.
- AI rules: the forum's rules don't mention AI, so we both show how work stays student-made and propose a tool-neutral AI-use policy and disclosure kit.
- Game to Gain may claim: story, world, consistent characters from sprite descriptions, soundtrack and sound effects (via music prompts), trailer and ad. Image generation only as "coming soon". Never claim export into a game engine.
- Phases: 1 instructors, 2 student pilot (2 academies, 20 students, ~10 projects, students choose the competition), 3 May round, 4 full season. Metrics: students trained, projects submitted, coaches certified. Periods are left as `[Period]` for Ibrahim to fill.
- Accounts: undecided. The two options are in the backups; Ibrahim chooses when asked.
- Pricing: today's public plans only, until Ibrahim changes it.
- Data protection, age and Face Swap appear in the speaker notes only.
