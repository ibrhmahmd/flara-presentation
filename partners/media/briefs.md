# Example video briefs

Make each film in Flara, export it as MP4, and save it here with the exact file name. The deck plays it on the example slide and shows a placeholder until the file exists. Keep each film short: the meeting needs proof, not a full entry. Update the slide title (`[film title]` and so on) in `../index.html`.

For every film, keep a note of what a student would do and what Flara did. You'll say this out loud on the slide.

## 1. Film Festival: `film-festival.mp4` (60–90 s excerpt)

- **Category:** Biographical (Arab scientists). Suggested subject: Ibn al-Haytham and the camera obscura. He works for the "science simplification" award too.
- **Logline (required by the rules):** "A boy in Cairo learns how light works by following Ibn al-Haytham's dark-room experiment, 1,000 years later."
- **Show:** a consistent lead character across 4–5 scenes (the present-day student, then Ibn al-Haytham in his era), Arabic narration with English subtitles or an English version, and an original soundtrack.
- **Student part to show:** the logline, and one character drawn by hand and animated with image to video.
- **Rubric it targets:** Storytelling 30, Creativity 30, sound and music within Camera 20.

## 2. Game to Gain: `game-to-gain.mp4` (45–60 s)

- **Topic:** "Arab scientist game". Suggested game: *Al-Jazari's Workshop*, a puzzle game about repairing his elephant clock.
- **Show:** a 30 s trailer (story hook, characters, the world, title card) and then a 15 s ad for the marketing plan ("Play it at [event]").
- **Use:** the team's sprite or character descriptions so the characters match the game, a soundtrack and a few sound effects from music prompts.
- **Don't show:** sprites or tiles exported into a game engine. Image generation is "coming soon" only.
- **Awards it targets:** Idea, Audio, Best Marketing, Audience vote.

## 3. Entrepreneur: `entrepreneur.mp4` (60–90 s excerpt of a ≤4 min pitch)

- **Suggested product:** a smart water bottle that reminds students to drink during exam season.
- **Show:** the story arc for investors (a problem moment, then the customer persona as an animated character, then the product in use, then a 10 s ad, then the ask), plus a voice-over and soundtrack.
- **Rules it follows:** "storytelling techniques"; "animation, soundtrack and/or special effects may be used"; must include an advertisement.
- **Student part to show:** the real prototype photo and the customer-interview quotes, which come from the students and not from AI.
