# Demo films: production plan

Make each film in Flara, export it as MP4 and save it in this folder with the exact file name given. The deck plays a file on its example slide and shows a placeholder until the file exists. For every film, write down **what the student made** and **what Flara did**: you say this out loud, and it is what the AI disclosure form would hold.

Keep films short. They are proof, not full entries.

## The idea: one game, one Flara deliverable

Each of Ibrahim's three games (deck slide 2) shows a different thing Game to Gain asks for, so together they cover the competition's awards:

## Same steps for every film

1. **Story:** paste the logline and let Flara draft the plot. Edit it, because the student's edits are the authorship.
2. **Character:** upload the game's own character art and use **image to video**. That makes "students draw, Flara animates" visible.
3. **Scenes:** 3–5 scenes from the game's own levels. Upload the level background as the reference so the film matches the game.
4. **Sound:** a soundtrack and 1–2 sound effects from the music studio, plus narration (text to speech) in Arabic, or English with an Arabic version.
5. **Export** the MP4 to this folder. Note the **tokens used**: that is the open item that sizes the Team Season Pass.

## 2. Crab Quest ad: `crab-quest-ad.mp4`

- **Hook:** "A crab escapes the kitchen and swims home through a polluted sea. Can you help her?"
- **Inputs** (in `~/Desktop/GD/Crab-game/images/`): `crab_medium.png` (the hero), `logo.png`, and the level backgrounds `level_1_bg.jpg` (kitchen), `level_3_bg.jpg` (sea floor) and `level_6_bg.png` (home).
- **Cut (15–20 s):** the crab on the kitchen counter → falling trash on the sea floor → reunited with her family → logo and "Play Crab Quest at AAFFIAT".
- **Sound:** an upbeat soundtrack, a bubble and wave sound effect, and one English voice-over line.
- **Point to make:** the marketing plan is its own award, and most teams don't have an ad.

## 3. Space X opening cutscene: `space-x-intro.mp4`

- **Mission line:** "An Egyptian astronaut flies to Europa to find out if its hidden ocean could hold life."
- **Inputs** (in `~/Desktop/GD/space-game/images/`): `characters/hero.png`, `characters/ship.png`, `ui/logo.png`, and the backgrounds `launch_pad`, `earth`, `belt`, `europa` and `jupiter`.
- **Scenes:** the Hero packs the Checklist (helmet, lucky football) → countdown and launch → the asteroid belt → Europa's ice → Jupiter rising. End on the logo.
- **Sound:** an orchestral launch soundtrack, a countdown voice, and a rocket sound effect.
- **Point to make:** the science facts become a story children remember, which fits "science simplification".

## 4. Film Festival: `film-festival.mp4`

- **Category:** Biographical (Arab scientists). Subject: Ibn al-Haytham and the camera obscura. It also targets the Best Science Simplification award.
- **Logline (the rules require one):** "A boy in Cairo learns how light works by following Ibn al-Haytham's dark-room experiment, 1,000 years later."
- **Show:** one consistent lead character across 4–5 scenes (the present-day boy, then Ibn al-Haytham in his era), Arabic narration with English subtitles, and an original soundtrack.
- **Student made:** the logline, and one hand-drawn character animated with image to video.
- **Rubric:** Storytelling 30, Creativity 30, and the sound and music part of Camera 20.

## 5. Entrepreneur: `entrepreneur.mp4`

- **Product:** a smart water bottle that reminds students to drink during exam season.
- **Story arc:** a problem moment (a tired student in an exam) → the customer persona as an animated character → the product in use → a 10 s ad → the ask.
- **Rules it follows:** "storytelling techniques"; "animation, soundtrack and/or special effects may be used"; it must include an advertisement.
- **Student made:** a real prototype photo and customer-interview quotes. These come from students, not from AI.
- **Option:** build it from a real Techno Future student idea instead, if you have one. That makes it stronger.
