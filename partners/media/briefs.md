## Part 1. Game to Gain: Flara features mapped to the rules

The game itself is judged. Flara never makes the game, its sprites or its levels. It makes what surrounds the game, and three of the seven awards plus the required video are about exactly that.

| Rule or award (from the PDF) | What the team needs | Flara studio |
|---|---|---|
| "A student must send video to his game before the competition date" | A game video | **Video Highlights**: cut recorded gameplay into a short reel. **Animated Short Films**: the story intro |
| Excellence in Idea | A clear story and world | **Story drafting** in Animated Short Films: logline, plot, characters |
| Excellence in Audio ("music and sound effects") | Music and SFX *inside the game* | **AI Music**: a soundtrack and short SFX prompts, exported as audio and played by the game |
| Best Marketing Award ("based on the marketing plan") | An ad and launch content | **Ads Generation**, **URL to Video** (from the game's web page if it has one) |
| Audience Award (public vote) | Something people share | The trailer, plus **Video Translation** for Arabic and English versions |
| Excellence in Visual Art | Judged on the game's own art | **Not Flara.** Only **Image to Video** animates the team's own art for the films. Don't claim more |
| Junior category 8–13 | — | Instructor-run account (Flara's AI providers are 13+) |

**Audio is the one place Flara output goes *into* the game.** Sound files are allowed to be claimed; sprite or art export is not (it's roadmap).

### Film A: Ibn Battuta, a game video and trailer: `game-to-gain.mp4` (top priority, for the 3 pm meeting)

- **Topic in the rules:** "Creative story". Ibn Battuta is a traveller and geographer, not a scientist, so don't file it under "Arab scientist game" unless the organisers agree.
- **Gap it fills:** the game's design doc says it has no opening cutscene and no ending.
- **Inputs** (`~/Desktop/GD/ibn-battota-game/`): `screenshots/01_start_screen.png` (the hero), `02_world_map.png`, and the gameplay shots `04_tangier`, `06_cairo`, `07_damascus` and `08_timbuktu`. Record 30–60 s of real gameplay with screen capture.
- **Flara steps:**
  1. **Story drafting:** logline "In 1325 a young man leaves Tangier for Mecca. He comes home thirty years later, with the world in his notebook." Draft a 5-line story, then edit it by hand.
  2. **Image to Video:** animate the start-screen Ibn Battuta (he turns, and walks toward the map).
  3. **Animated Short Films:** 3 scenes with the same character: Tangier at dawn → Cairo's minarets and the Nile → Damascus with scholars.
  4. **Video Highlights:** upload the gameplay recording and get a 15 s highlight cut.
  5. **AI Music:** an oud adventure theme (also save it as the game's menu music), plus 2 SFX: a city-arrival chime and a coin pickup.
  6. **Text-to-Speech:** one Arabic diary line ("I left Tangier alone, like a bird leaving its nest"). **Video Translation:** an English version.
- **Edit (40–50 s):** animated intro → map → gameplay highlights → title card "The Adventures of Ibn Battuta · AAFFIAT Game to Gain".
- **Student made:** the game, the code, the city order, the start-screen art and the edits to the story. **Flara:** the animation, highlight cut, music, SFX and voice.

### Film B: Crab Quest, a marketing ad: `crab-quest-ad.mp4`

- **Topic in the rules:** "Creative story" (it also carries an ocean-pollution message).
- **Inputs** (`~/Desktop/GD/Crab-game/`): `images/crab_medium.png`, `images/logo.png`, and the backgrounds `level_1_bg.jpg` (kitchen), `level_3_bg.jpg` (sea floor) and `level_6_bg.png` (home); `music/background_music.mp3` is the current track. Record 20 s of gameplay.
- **Flara steps:**
  1. **Ads Generation:** a 15–20 s ad. Hook: "A crab escapes the kitchen and swims home through a polluted sea. Can you get it home?"
  2. **Image to Video:** animate the crab sprite and the logo for the ad's first and last shots.
  3. **AI Music:** an upbeat ocean theme to replace `background_music.mp3`, plus SFX for bubbles, a wave and a trash hit. **This targets Excellence in Audio.**
  4. **Video Translation:** Arabic and English versions of the ad.
  5. **(Optional) URL to Video:** if the game has a web or itch.io page, make a second ad from it.
- **Marketing plan one-pager** (for the Best Marketing award, written by the student): who the players are, the message, where the ad runs (the academy's page, parents' WhatsApp groups, the forum's audience vote), and the launch date.
- **Student made:** the game, the hook line and the marketing plan. **Flara:** the ad, the animation, the music and SFX, and the translation.

## Part 2. Film Festival

Moved to its own folder: `../../film-festival/README.md`. The meeting excerpt is saved here as `film-festival.mp4`.

---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Ages 9–16, Egypt | An academy team of 13–16 year-olds (student accounts possible); younger teams use an instructor-run account |
| 2.1 | MP4 or MOV | Flara exports MP4 |
| 2.2 | A logline | Written by the students (below) |
| 2.3 | 3–10 minutes | Target 4–5 min; the meeting demo is a 60–90 s excerpt |
| 2.4 / 3 | One category | **3.1 Biographical Films (documentary)**: the lives and discoveries of Arab scientists |
| 4.1 | "Primarily created by young filmmakers, with minimal adult assistance" | Students write, film, record and edit. Flara only makes the scenes they can't film |
| 4.2 / 8.1 | Outside shots allowed, but they must be attributed, with how they influenced the film | The AI-use disclosure: every Flara scene is listed in the end credits and on the form |

### Scoring and where points come from

| Criterion | Points | Who earns them |
|---|---|---|
| Creativity | 30 | Students: the concept of linking a modern student with a historical scientist. Flara: visuals and settings of the 11th century |
| Storytelling | 30 | Students: the plot, the message, the narration script. Flara: Story drafting as a first draft only |
| Camera work (including sound and music) | 20 | **Students must film real shots**, or this scores low. Flara: the soundtrack |
| Editing and pacing | 10 | Students edit in a normal editor (CapCut, DaVinci) |
| Visual effects | 10 | Flara's animated scenes, blended with the live action |

**Key point for the forum:** a fully AI film would lose most of the camera-work points and break rule 4.1. The winning pattern is **real footage by students plus Flara for what can't be filmed**. That is also the honest answer to "AI did it".

### The film: "The Dark Room" (Biographical, Ibn al-Haytham)

- **Logline:** "A student in Cairo builds a camera from a cardboard box and finds out a scientist from Basra explained how it works 1,000 years ago." The real camera obscura experiment also fits the Best Science Simplification award.
- **Structure (about 4.5 min):**

| Part | Time | Filmed by students | Made in Flara |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0:00–0:40 | A student builds a pinhole box camera at home; an upside-down image appears | Soundtrack (AI Music) |
| 2. Question | 0:40–1:10 | The student asks "who first explained this?", filmed with a phone | — |
| 3. Back in time | 1:10–3:00 | — | **Animated Short Films**: Ibn al-Haytham in Cairo, about 1011; the dark-room experiment; writing the *Book of Optics*. Consistent character across 4 scenes. **Text-to-Speech**: narration in Arabic |
| 4. Legacy | 3:00–3:50 | The student films a modern camera and eye diagrams from a school book | Flara: one animated transition from his diagram to a modern camera lens |
| 5. Close | 3:50–4:30 | The student repeats the experiment for classmates | Music; **Video Translation** for an English subtitled version |
| Credits | — | Team names | "AI scenes made with Flara" and a list of the scenes (rules 4.2 and 8.1) |

- **Image to Video option:** a student draws Ibn al-Haytham by hand, and Flara animates the drawing as the character's first appearance. This is the strongest "the student made it" moment.
- **Fact check before release:** dates and places of Ibn al-Haytham's life (Basra → Cairo, *Kitāb al-Manāẓir*) against a reliable source.
- **For the meeting:** a 60–90 s excerpt made of part 1 (a quick phone shot) plus 2 Flara scenes from part 3, with narration.
