## Decisions (8 Nov 2026)

- **Two canvases** for Rights Hero leadership: Flara today (facts only) and Flara Education Egypt, year 1 (2026–27 season). LEGO's version of each block goes in the speaker notes, not on the slides.
- **Deck order:** today canvas, Education canvas, 9 Education block slides (each says what changes from today), and an A/B payer slide after Revenue.
- **Education headline:** "Flara in every round and every course". The goal is that Flara gets used in every competition round and every course. It isn't a revenue target.

### Flara Education Egypt, year 1

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Customer segments | Private STEM academies: the owner buys, the instructors use it. Students and teams aged 6–18 are the users. Competitions aren't a segment. |
| Value: academies | Results parents can see; stronger competition entries; a new course to sell; Flara itself for the academy's own marketing materials |
| Value: students | Their story becomes a film; storytelling skill; a competition-ready entry; safe, disclosed AI use |
| Channels: reach | Ibrahim's direct network; introductions through AAFFIAT; forum events and showcases; student films on social media |
| Channels: deliver | The flara.io web app; onboarding sessions with instructors; co-designed course content; guides and templates for each competition |
| Relationships | A dedicated partner manager (Ibrahim); the competition season cycle (February, May and August rounds); a review after each round. Students are reached only through the instructor. |
| Revenue | **A:** an Enterprise contract for instructor accounts. **B:** the same Enterprise contract plus student seats, with the cost built into the course fee. Token top-ups. The pilot uses free accounts. |
| Key resources | The Flara platform; Ibrahim's competition know-how; the course and guide library; the example films |
| Key activities | Partner management; instructor onboarding; content creation |
| Key partners | The AAFFIAT forum (no money either way); the pilot academies |
| Costs | AI generation per film; Ibrahim's time on sales and onboarding |

### Flara today

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Customer segments | Individual creators; marketers and small businesses; agencies and companies (Enterprise) |
| Value | One studio from idea to film; consistent characters; music, voice and translation; full Arabic support |
| Channels | Website and self sign-up; social media and content; direct sales for Enterprise; Flara MCP |
| Relationships | Self-service; the Free plan as the entry point |
| Revenue | Free $0, Starter $4.99/mo, Creator $19.98/mo, Enterprise custom |
| Key resources | The platform (nine studios); model integrations |
| Key activities | Product development; model integration; marketing |
| Key partners | AI model providers; cloud and payment providers |
| Costs | Model usage per generation; the product team and marketing |
