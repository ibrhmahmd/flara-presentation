Decision needed

Who holds the accounts: instructors or students?

A · Instructor accounts

  • The academy pays for the tool
  • Instructors run Flara with each team
  • Works for under-13s
  • No student data in Flara

B · Student accounts

  • The academy buys seats and adds them to the course fee
  • Each team has its own login and AI log
  • Only for students aged 13+
  • Needs consent and the data licence

Recommendation: [A or B, to decide].

Same split as backups B1 and B2 in the partners deck. The AI providers behind Flara set 13+, and most Film Festival and Game to Gain juniors are under 13. B needs guardian consent and the data-protection licence (Law 151/2020) first. Watch the Film Festival rule on "minimal adult assistance" under A.
